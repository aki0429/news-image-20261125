# Sources — every story as reported on Wednesday 25 November 2026

All stories were reported on 25 November 2026 (the Riyadh strike and the Huawei
verdict landed late on 24 November in their local time zones, i.e. on
25 November across Asia-Pacific time zones, and led the day's coverage).

- **Riyadh PIF Tower strike** — AFP via Channel NewsAsia ("Saudi skyscraper hit
  again, coalition slams 'terrorist attack'"), Al Jazeera ("Saudi civil defence
  says 11 injured in Houthi drone attack on Riyadh tower"), WSJ, Dawn
  (published 25 Nov 2026), SCMP. Facts used: 80-storey Public Investment Fund
  Tower, 385 m, King Abdullah Financial District; second strike in a week
  (first on 19 Nov); 11 injured (2 Saudis, 2 Indians, 3 Nepalis, 4 Egyptians);
  coalition spokesman called it a "cowardly terrorist attack"; hostilities
  with the Houthis restarted in July.
- **Markets / tariffs** — Reuters, "Stocks retreat as AI rebound stumbles,
  tariff threats loom" (25 Nov 2026): MSCI Asia-Pacific ex-Japan −2.3%, Kospi
  −5.3% at worst, Taiwan −5.1%, Nikkei −2.9%, Nvidia −5.9%, Brent $90.97,
  Bitcoin $59,257. Reuters (24 Nov, sources): Trump administration poised to
  release Section 301 "excess capacity" probe findings on 16 trading partners,
  new duties expected.
- **Huawei conviction** — US Department of Justice press releases (Office of
  Public Affairs and EDNY) and Reuters/CNN (24 Nov 2026): federal jury in
  Brooklyn convicted Huawei and three subsidiaries on 10 of 12 counts,
  including bank/wire fraud, IEEPA sanctions violations over Iran, conspiracy
  to steal trade secrets and money laundering conspiracy; acquitted of
  racketeering conspiracy and obstruction conspiracy; trial lasted ~3 months;
  sentencing set for 14 Sep 2027.
- **Shenzhou-24** — The Japan Times and Telegraph India (25 Nov 2026): China
  launched Shenzhou-24 with the first Pakistani national/astronaut aboard,
  bound for the Tiangong space station.
- **Indonesia mine rescue** — Reuters (25 Nov 2026): rescuers in West Java
  searching for possibly dozens (local media: ~30) of workers trapped since
  Monday in an unauthorised gold mine in the Pongkor area of Bogor, ~100 m
  deep, flooded by heavy rain.
- **UK inflation** — ONS figures reported 25 Nov 2026 (Moneycontrol): CPI rose
  3.6% year-on-year in October, up from 3.5% in September, a one-year high and
  a fourth consecutive increase, driven by fuel and energy costs.
- **Australia apology** — Reuters (25 Nov 2026, 01:16 UTC): the Australian
  government issued a formal apology to current and former LGBTQ service
  members for decades of discriminatory policies; Defence Minister Richard
  Marles addressed parliament.
- **Colombia hostages** — SCMP world desk (25 Nov 2026): gunmen held hostages
  in a Cartagena supermarket after a failed armoured-car raid; police and
  troops surrounded the store.
- **Landmine Monitor** — SCMP (25 Nov 2026): global landmine/ERW casualties
  hit a five-year high of 7,000, with Myanmar and Syria topping the list.
- **CIA breach** — SCMP and Telegraph India (25 Nov 2026): an unclassified CIA
  system was breached in a cyberattack; no classified data compromised.
